Quick Guide & FAQ

1. NFS Underground 2 Save File Location (Windows 10 / 11)

Need for Speed Underground 2 career save files are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NFS Underground 2 (or C:\Users\<Username>\AppData\Local\NFS Underground 2). Click Copy Path in the top bar, press Win + R, paste the path, and open your profile subfolder.

2. How to Get Rachel's 350Z & Caleb's GTO in Career Mode

Normally, Rachel's green Nissan 350Z is only drivable in the prologue and Caleb's Pontiac GTO is unobtainable. With our Car Injector, select any garage slot and choose Rachel's Nissan 350Z or Caleb's Pontiac GTO (Final Boss) to own and drive them permanently in Career Mode.

3. NFSU2 Money Trainer Online (Unlimited Career Cash)

No need to download risky .exe memory trainers or Cheat Engine tables. Load your career save file in the browser, click Max Cash ($99,999,999) or type any custom bank balance, and download your updated save file instantly.

4. Safe Career Add-ons vs. 100% Save Game Unlock

Safe Career Add-ons unlock Widebody kits, TJ unique parts, all 25 Car Lot vehicles, and hidden shop map icons while keeping your active storyline races intact. 100% Sandbox Mode applies a full 100% completed world (Stage 5, all 5 garage slots, all shops) while preserving your name, cash, and cars.

5. How to Unlock Widebody Kits & Unique TJ Upgrades

In standard career mode, Widebody kits and TJ's Unique performance upgrades require winning specific Outrun races across Stages 3–5. Click Special Parts under Safe Career Add-ons to unlock all Widebody kits, unique hoods, rims, and sponsor vinyls on any save.

6. How to Unlock All 5 Garage Slots in Career Mode

Career garage slots 2 through 5 are unlocked by signing sponsor contracts in Stages 1–5. Click Enable All 5 Slots or Apply 100% Complete World so the game engine activates all 5 sponsor garage slots for your profile.

7. Player Builds & Custom Dyno Tuning

Vehicles under Player Builds (such as the Mazda RX-7 Custom Special, David Choe AE86, and Audi A3 Custom) come pre-equipped with custom visual styling and maxed-out ECU/Turbo dyno tables for top-tier acceleration.

8. Instant Level 3 Extreme Performance Tuning

Each occupied garage slot includes a dedicated Perf Tier selector. Switch any vehicle between Stock (0%), Street (Lvl 1), Pro (Lvl 2), and Extreme (Lvl 3 Max) to install all 68 performance packages in one click.

9. Import & Export Custom .JSON Car Patches

Want to move your favorite build from one career save to another or share it with friends? Click Export .json on any slot to save its bodykit, paint, vinyls, and tuning, then click Import .json on any other save file.

Produces standard 54,966-byte (20CM header) career save binaries compatible with PC retail versions (v1.1 / v1.2), Widescreen Fix setups, and browser-based WebAssembly ports. Enable Auto .bak for automatic backups.
