Quick Guide & FAQ

Need for Speed Underground 2 Save Editor — Quick Guide & FAQ

1. NFS Underground 2 Save File Location (Windows 10 / 11)

Need for Speed Underground 2 career save files are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NFS Underground 2 (or C:\Users\<Username>\AppData\Local\NFS Underground 2). Click Copy Path in the top bar, press Win + R, paste the path, and open your profile subfolder.

Read the save file location guide →

2. How to Get Rachel's 350Z & Caleb's GTO in Career Mode

Normally, Rachel's green Nissan 350Z is only drivable in the prologue and Caleb's Pontiac GTO is unobtainable. Select any garage slot and choose Rachel's Nissan 350Z or Caleb's Pontiac GTO (Final Boss) from the preset menu to own and drive them permanently in Career Mode.

Read the Rachel's 350Z & Caleb's GTO guide →

3. NFSU2 Money Trainer Online (Unlimited Career Cash)

No need to download risky .exe memory trainers or Cheat Engine tables. Load your career save file in the browser, click Max Cash ($99,999,999) or type any custom bank balance, and download your updated save file instantly.

Read the money trainer guide →

4. Safe Career Add-ons vs. 100% Save Game Unlock

Safe Career Add-ons fill the per-car part unlock tables and mark perf parts as owned without changing your career stage or races (Reveal Shops opens every shop your stage allows). 100% Career instead completes every stage and event (stage 6), keeps your cars and name, and raises cash to at least $777,777.

5. How Outrun Unique Parts & Widebody Kits Work

In Career mode, TJ's 9 Outrun Unique performance upgrades (Weight, Trans, NOS, Engine, Turbo, Susp, Brakes, ECU, Tires) and Widebody kits are stored inside each vehicle slot rather than on permanent shop shelves. Use the 🏆 Outrun Uniques toggles inside any slot card to install all 9 Uniques directly onto your car!

Read the Outrun Unique parts guide →

6. How to Unlock All 5 Garage Slots in Career Mode

Click Enable All 5 Slots in the Career Garage header. It clears the lock byte (+0x428) of each locked slot, the same change the game makes when you earn a slot, so all 5 slots work even on Stage 1 saves. Your career stage and races are not changed.

Read the GARAGE.DAT & garage slots guide →

7. Per-Slot Dyno Curve Presets (Power / Drag / Drift)

Each occupied slot includes a Dyno Preset selector that tunes all 5 ECU & Turbo torque setups (Circuit, Drag, Drift, Street X, URL) for Peak Horsepower, Drag Acceleration, or Drift mid-range torque without breaking your gearbox ratios.

8. Conflict-Free Level 3 Extreme Performance

Switching a vehicle's Perf Tier to Extreme (Lvl 3 Max) installs all non-conflicting Stage 3 performance packages, preserves your visual aero bumpers/spoilers, and sets the 9-byte shop ownership bitmask so parts appear as Owned in shops.

9. Import & Export Custom .JSON Car Patches

Want to move your favorite build from one career save to another or share it with friends? Click Export .json on any slot to save its bodykit, paint, vinyls, and tuning, then click Import .json on any other save file.

10. How to Edit Saves from nfsu2.xyz (Web Port) & GARAGE.DAT

Export from nfsu2.xyz: Download your save archive (.zip), open NFS Underground 2 → <Your Profile Folder>, and drop the file matching your profile name into this editor (for Slots 6–10, switch to the GARAGE.DAT (6–10) tab and load GARAGE.DAT).
Import back to nfsu2.xyz: Click Restore on nfsu2.xyz, select your edited save file (or GARAGE.DAT), and confirm replacement.

11. NFS Underground 2 PC Cheat Codes (Title Screen)

Type these codes at the "Press Enter" title screen before loading your profile: regmebaby ($20,000 starter cash in career), ordermebaby ($1,000 in career + unlocks Skyline/RX-8 in Quick Race), opendoors (unlocks all city areas), gimmevisual1 & gimmevisual2 (visual upgrades), needperformance1 & needperformance2 (performance tiers).

Read the PC cheat codes list →

12. Automatic Header CRC-32 Recalculation

Every downloaded save file automatically recalculates all three MSB-first CRC-32 checksums (Block A at 0x10, Block B at 0x14, and Header at 0x18) . Checksums of the loaded file are verified and a warning is shown if the original was already damaged.
