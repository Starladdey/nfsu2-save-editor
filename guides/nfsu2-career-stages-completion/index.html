Stage 0 is the prologue (it also has 2 story events that are not races). After stage 5 the save switches to stage 6, which means "career complete".

How the completion % is counted

The statistics screen counts the main events you have won out of 224. Photo goals are separate and do not change the percentage. That is why a stage can be "done" long before its percentage looks full: a stage ends after its final event, and the other races stay on the map to be won later.

Frequently asked questions

How many races are in NFSU2?

The career has 224 main events: 154 races, 24 sponsor events, 44 URL events and 2 story events, plus 30 photo goals.

Why does my stage say done when races are left?

A stage ends after its final event. The other races of that stage stay on the map and still count towards the percentage.

Do photo goals count towards 100%?

No. The percentage only counts won main events. Photo goals are tracked separately.
