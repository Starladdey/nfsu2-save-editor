NFSU2 Hidden Shops — When They Unlock and How to Find Them

Updated November 13, 2026 · by Starladdey

Shops in Need for Speed Underground 2 are not all on the map from the start. They open as your career moves on, and most of them stay hidden until you drive past. Here is how it works and how to reveal every shop your stage allows.

How shops unlock

The city has 22 shops for parts, paint and visuals. A shop can be used when two things are true:

  • Your stage allows it. Every shop has a minimum career stage, and 6 of them also wait for a specific event to be won.
  • You have discovered it. 18 of the 22 shops must be found first: drive past and the game marks it on the map. The other 4 are open as soon as their stage is reached.

Reveal every shop your stage allows

  1. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor and load your save.
  2. In Safe Career Add-ons, click Reveal Shops. The editor marks every shop your stage allows as discovered, exactly as the game does when you drive past.
  3. Download and replace the save: the shop icons are on the map.

Shops of later stages stay hidden, and your races and stage do not change. The overview shows how many shops are open (for example 19 / 22), and Progress by stage lists them per stage.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a shop still missing after Reveal Shops?

It belongs to a later stage, or it waits for a specific event to be won first. It opens as your career moves on, or with 100% Career.

Does Reveal Shops change my career?

No. It only marks shops as discovered. Stage, races and their results stay the same.

Can I open all 22 shops at once?

Yes, by finishing the career with 100% Career: every stage is reached, and every shop with it.
