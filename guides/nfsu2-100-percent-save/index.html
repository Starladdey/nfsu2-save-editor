What 100% means in NFSU2

The game's statistics screen ("Game Completion Status") counts the main career events you have won: 224 of them make 100%. On top of that the career has 30 photo goals (magazine and DVD covers) and six stages. When the last stage is finished, the save switches to stage 6, the "career complete" state.

How to finish the career on your own save

  1. Close the game and copy your profile folder somewhere safe (see the save file location guide).
  2. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor and load your save (or the .zip backup from nfsu2.xyz).
  3. Click Apply 100% Career. The button turns green: click it again to confirm.
  4. Check Save File Overview: Completion 100%, Main Events Won 224 / 224, Photo Goals 30 / 30, Career Complete.
  5. Click Download Save, check the list of changes and replace the file in your profile folder.

After 100%

Dealer cars open because the events that unlock them are won (see how to unlock all cars), and every shop of the city becomes available. Want a different car right away? Put Rachel's 350Z or a boss car into a free slot.

Frequently asked questions

Does 100% Career keep my cars?

Yes. Only career progress, garage slot locks and cash change. Your cars, their parts, paint and tuning stay as they were.

Can I undo it?

Before you download, yes: press Undo (Ctrl+Z). After you replace the file, restore the copy of your profile folder.

Does it work with nfsu2.xyz?

Yes. Load the .zip backup, apply 100% Career, download the .zip and restore it on nfsu2.xyz.
