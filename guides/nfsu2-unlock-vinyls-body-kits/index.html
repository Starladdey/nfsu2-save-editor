How to Unlock All Vinyls, Body Kits and Specialties in NFSU2

Updated November 13, 2026 · by Starladdey

In Need for Speed Underground 2 visual parts unlock as you progress, separately for every car. The NFSU2 Save Editor can open them all without touching your career, so you keep every race to play.

What can be unlocked

Each car in your garage has its own unlock lists, and the editor opens them by group:

  • Vinyls & Decals: vinyls, decals and paint options.
  • Body Parts: kits, bumpers, spoilers, hoods, rims and similar.
  • Specialties: neon, doors, audio, hydraulics and similar.

How to unlock them

  1. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor and load your save.
  2. In Safe Career Add-ons, click Vinyls & Decals, Body Parts and/or Specialties. Each works on all five cars of the open garage (slots 1–5, or 6–10 on the GARAGE.DAT tab).
  3. Download and replace the save. The parts are in the shops.

Your stage and races stay exactly as they were. Want only vinyls and keep earning body kits? Use just that one button.

Things the game still checks

  • Nitrous Purge only shows up once the car has a nitrous system installed.
  • Level 3 performance parts appear in the performance shop only when your career reaches the stage that unlocks them. Some visual options, like engine bay neon, ask for a level 3 engine: buy the level 3 engine package in that shop once it is there, because setting the tuning level in the editor is not enough for this check.
  • Outrun Uniques are separate: see the Outrun Unique parts guide.

Frequently asked questions

Will unlocking parts skip my career?

No. The add-ons only open part lists for your cars. Stage, races and results do not change.

Why is Nitrous Purge still locked?

It needs a nitrous system installed on the car. Buy or install nitrous first.

Do I have to unlock everything at once?

No. Vinyls & Decals, Body Parts and Specialties are separate buttons.
