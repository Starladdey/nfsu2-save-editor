How to Get Rachel's 350Z and Caleb's Pontiac GTO in NFSU2 Career

Updated November 7, 2026 · by Starladdey

In the normal game, Rachel's green Nissan 350Z is only drivable during the prologue and Caleb's Pontiac GTO, the final boss car, cannot be owned at all. With a save editor you can place either car into one of your career garage slots and drive it permanently.

Add Rachel's 350Z or Caleb's GTO to your garage

  1. Close the game and back up your profile folder (see the save file location guide).
  2. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor and load your career save.
  3. In the Career Garage panel, choose a slot. If slots are locked, click Enable All 5 Slots first.
  4. Open the Car Preset dropdown and select Rachel's Nissan 350Z or Caleb's Pontiac GTO (Final Boss).
  5. Optionally add Outrun Unique parts and set Perf: Extreme (Level 3 Max).
  6. Click Download Save and replace the file in your profile folder.
Heads-up: a preset writes the car into the slot you picked, replacing what is there. Choose an empty slot, or export the current car as .json first if you want to keep it.

Other story and sponsor cars

The editor ships with 40+ built-in car presets, not just Rachel's 350Z and Caleb's GTO: story and sponsor vehicles such as Nikki's Mustang, Snoop Dogg's Escalade and Capone's H2, custom rivals, player builds, regional exclusives, and all 26 stock career cars. Every preset is installed the same way, through the Car Preset dropdown of a garage slot.

Regional exclusive cars

Region-specific cars are available from the Factory Stock Career Vehicles list in any garage slot: Honda Civic Si Coupe [US], Acura RSX Type-S [US], Peugeot 106 [EU] and Opel/Vauxhall Corsa [EU].

Move a build between saves

Click Export .json on any slot to save its body kit, paint, vinyls and tuning, then use Import .json on another save file to drop the same build into it. It is also an easy way to share builds with friends.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really keep these cars in career mode?

Yes. Once the car is written into a garage slot and you copy the edited save back, it is part of your career garage and you can own and drive it permanently.

Does choosing a preset delete my old car?

A preset replaces the car in the slot you selected. Pick an empty slot, or export the existing car to .json first so you can import it again later.

Which other story and sponsor cars are included?

Besides Rachel's 350Z and Caleb's GTO, the presets include Nikki's Mustang, Snoop Dogg's Escalade and Capone's H2, plus custom rivals, player builds, regional exclusives and all 26 stock career cars.

My garage slots are locked. What now?

Click Enable All 5 Slots in the Career Garage header. It clears the slot lock flags so all five slots work, even on a Stage 1 save.
