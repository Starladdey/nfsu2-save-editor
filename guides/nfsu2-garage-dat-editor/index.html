NFSU2 GARAGE.DAT Editor — Unlock All 5 Slots and Edit Slots 6–10

Updated November 12, 2026 · by Starladdey

The Need for Speed Underground 2 career garage has five sponsor slots, and extended 10-car garages keep slots 6 to 10 in a separate GARAGE.DAT file. The online editor handles both, right in your browser.

Unlock all 5 career garage slots

Click Enable All 5 Slots in the Career Garage header. It clears the lock byte (+0x428) of each locked slot, the same change the game makes when you earn a slot, so all five slots work even on a Stage 1 save. Your career stage and races are not changed.

Edit extended slots 6–10 (GARAGE.DAT)

  1. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor.
  2. Load your career save first, then switch the garage panel to the GARAGE.DAT (6–10) tab and load the GARAGE.DAT from the same profile folder. Using nfsu2.xyz? Load the whole .zip backup instead: both files come with it.
  3. Edit the cars in slots 6–10 just like the regular slots: presets, Outrun Uniques, Dyno presets and .json import or export.
  4. Click Download Save. You get the save and, when slots 6–10 changed, GARAGE.DAT as well (or one edited .zip if you loaded a .zip). Replace the files in your profile folder.

GARAGE.DAT stores a fingerprint of the career save, so the editor re-links it to your edited save and downloads both whenever needed. GARAGE.DAT files for slots 6–10 are used by nfsu2.xyz and the Extra Options mod.

Using saves from nfsu2.xyz

Export from nfsu2.xyz: in Saves, download a backup (.zip) and drop the whole .zip into the editor. Choose your profile in the list; its GARAGE.DAT (slots 6–10) loads with it.

Import back to nfsu2.xyz: click Download Save to get a .zip with only the profiles you edited, then restore that .zip on nfsu2.xyz. Your other profiles and nfsu2.xyz settings stay as they are. GARAGE.DAT is restored only from the archive, inside the profile folder.

Frequently asked questions

Does Enable All 5 Slots work on a new save?

Yes. It clears the lock byte of each locked career slot, the same change the game makes when you earn a slot, so all five slots work even on a Stage 1 save. Your career stage and races are not changed.

Which files do I download after editing?

Click Download Save. If you loaded a .zip archive, you get one edited .zip. If you loaded the save and GARAGE.DAT separately, you get the save, plus GARAGE.DAT whenever slots 6 to 10 changed or it had to be re-linked to the edited save. Replace both files in the profile folder.

Can I edit saves exported from nfsu2.xyz?

Yes. Drop the whole .zip backup from nfsu2.xyz into the editor, pick the profile, edit it and download the .zip, then restore that .zip on nfsu2.xyz. Slots 6 to 10 come back only from the archive, where GARAGE.DAT sits in the profile folder.
