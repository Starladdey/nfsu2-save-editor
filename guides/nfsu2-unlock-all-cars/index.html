How to Unlock All Cars in NFS Underground 2

Updated November 13, 2026 · by Starladdey

In Need for Speed Underground 2 the car dealer does not unlock cars by money or by a hidden switch in the save. Each locked car waits for a specific career event to be won. Here is how that works and the two ways around it.

How the dealer unlocks cars

The game has 24 dealer cars with an unlock condition. Each one is tied to a career event: when that event is won, the car appears at the dealer. For 17 of them the event is the same everywhere; for 7 it depends on the region version of the game. A few cars are regional exclusives: the Honda Civic Si Coupe and Acura RSX Type-S (US), the Peugeot 106 and Opel/Vauxhall Corsa (EU).

Because the dealer checks won events, there is no save "table" that unlocks all cars on its own. Older versions of this editor had a Car Lot button that wrote such a table; tests in the game showed it did nothing, so it was removed in v3.0.

Option 1: win the events (or finish the career)

Playing the career opens the cars one by one. If you just want them all, 100% Career completes every event, and the dealer cars of your region open with it. Your own cars and name are kept.

Option 2: put any car straight into your garage

  1. Open the NFSU2 Save Editor and load your save.
  2. Pick a free slot (click Enable All 5 Slots if they are locked).
  3. Choose a car in the slot's car list: all 31 stock cars, regional exclusives, story and sponsor cars (Nikki's Mustang, Snoop Dogg's Escalade, Capone's H2), boss cars like Caleb's GTO, or Rachel's 350Z.
  4. Download the save and replace the file in your profile folder.

This works at any stage and does not touch your races. Story and boss cars come fully tuned; stock cars come stock.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a save edit that unlocks all dealer cars?

Not as a separate switch: the dealer checks which career events are won. Finishing the career (100% Career) opens them; putting a car into a garage slot works at any stage.

Why is one dealer car still locked after 100%?

Some cars are tied to a different event in the other region version of the game, or are regional exclusives.

Can I get the regional exclusives in my region?

Yes, by putting them straight into a garage slot from the car list.
