About the editor

The NFSU2 Save Editor edits career cash, profile name, garage slots 1–10 (including GARAGE.DAT), Outrun Uniques, Dyno presets, shop discovery and story cars. All three header CRC-32 checksums are recalculated automatically so the game accepts the edited file, and nothing is ever uploaded: your save stays in your browser.
